# Ken Research — Verified Fact Sheet
_Last researched: 2026-04-28 by Atlas (Sonnet research agent)_

## Snapshot

Ken Research is a privately held Indian market research and strategy consulting firm with offices in Gurugram (HQ), Dubai, Tangerang (Indonesia), and Doha. Incorporated in 2012 as KEN RESEARCH PRIVATE LIMITED, the company sells syndicated market reports, custom research projects, strategy consulting, primary surveys, and subscription intelligence products to corporate clients across 14+ industries globally. Its stated positioning is as an affordable, consulting-led alternative to large Western research houses, with particular geographic emphasis on GCC/Middle East, South and Southeast Asia, and Africa. MCA-reported revenue for FY2024 was ₹11.1 crore (~$1.3M USD), making it a small-to-mid boutique by global standards despite large catalog claims. LinkedIn shows a 201–500 headcount range; MCA filings indicate 62 employees as of October 2024 — a meaningful discrepancy discussed below.

## Service Lines

Ken Research organizes its offering under five branded product lines as of 2025/2026:

### 1. Ken Consulting
Strategy consulting services. Sub-offerings: go-to-market strategy, market entry analysis, market penetration advisory, product strategy, deals & IPO advisory (M&A readiness, IPO preparation). Uses a proprietary "Ken Connect" platform claimed to support data-driven M&A, IPO readiness, and go-to-market execution. (source: kenresearch.com homepage)

### 2. Ken Intelligence
Syndicated and custom market research reports. Claims catalog of 100,000+ reports spanning 1,500+ industries. Also aggregates third-party publishers through the Dossier 360 subscription product (see Pricing). Includes industry reports, company profiles, and geographic market analyses. (source: kenresearch.com homepage, marketresearch.com)

### 3. Ken Survey
Primary research / Voice of Customer (VoC). Offers quantitative and qualitative surveys across 70+ countries. Claims 7,000+ surveys delivered across 1,000+ industries with 250,000+ respondents. (source: kenresearch.com homepage)

### 4. Ken Procurement
Supply chain intelligence, risk-controlled procurement frameworks, vendor scouting, supplier benchmarking, cost-saving analysis, and contract negotiation support. (source: kenresearch.com/procurement-research)

### 5. Ken Expert Panel
Network of 180,000+ vetted industry experts for primary expert interviews and on-ground intelligence. Used to support consulting engagements and custom research. (source: kenresearch.com homepage)

### Subscription product: Dossier 360
A subscription-based intelligence platform offering access to 10,000+ premium industry reports, press releases, investment/economy news, and 30,000+ equity and private company reports from 100+ publishers. Plan tiers: Single, Site, and Corporate [unverified - single source: kenresearch.com blog, 2020]. Specific pricing not publicly disclosed. (source: kenresearch.com/dossier360.php — page returned 500 error at time of research; older blog posts describe the product)
