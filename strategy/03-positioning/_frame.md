## The category problem (hypothesis to validate)

Most global market-research firms compete on:
- Number of reports published
- Sectors covered
- SEO surface area for "[market] size 2026"
- Volume-discounted pricing

Almost none compete on:
- The experience of *consuming* a report
- The experience of *finding* the right report
- The experience of *trusting* the firm before purchase
- The experience of *living with* the data after purchase (dashboards, updates, alerts, exports)
- The experience of *being a client* (proposals, kickoff, delivery, follow-up)

If that hypothesis holds after the competitor audit, **experience is the wedge**.

## The five surfaces where experience is decided

1. **Discovery surface** — Google → landing page → "is this firm credible in 5 seconds?"
2. **Report store surface** — catalog → search → sample → checkout. Where most rivals hemorrhage trust.
3. **Report consumption surface** — viewer/PDF/online reader. Where everyone in the category is bad.
4. **Dashboard / data surface** — live data, filters, exports. Where CB Insights and PitchBook win and nobody else shows up.
5. **Engagement surface** — proposals, custom-research onboarding, ongoing relationship. Where consulting firms beat research firms.

We will write a sub-doc per surface in [`04-experience-playbook/`](../04-experience-playbook/) once the audit is in.
