# Deep-Dive Round 2 — Page-Level UX Receipts

_Atlas, 2026-04-28. Round 2 follow-up to the competitor landscape map._

## Why this round exists
The original competitor research ([`../competitor-landscape.md`](../competitor-landscape.md)) is breadth — 20 firms, strategic positioning, scoring. Useful for picking the wedge, weak for *page-by-page* design execution. This round is depth: 5 chosen rivals walked page by page, mobile + desktop, with sourced URLs, so the playbook in [`../../04-experience-playbook/`](../../04-experience-playbook/) can reference specific moves to copy or beat — not just principles.

## The 5 chosen
| # | Company | Why chosen | File |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mordor Intelligence | Direct Tier A rival, decent within Tier A | [`mordor-intelligence.md`](mordor-intelligence.md) |
| 2 | IMARC Group | Direct Tier A rival, weaker — shows the bottom of category | [`imarc-group.md`](imarc-group.md) |
| 3 | Statista (reports) | The only Tier A firm with Tier C UX. Closest thing to our wedge. | [`statista-reports.md`](statista-reports.md) |
| 4 | CB Insights | Workflow-first product framing, AI-native | [`cb-insights.md`](cb-insights.md) |
| 5 | Crunchbase | Best homepage UX in the category (per Round 1 audit) | [`crunchbase.md`](crunchbase.md) |

We deliberately did NOT deep-dive the other 15. Most share the same Tier A failure pattern (no pricing, no preview, form-fill funnel, navy palette). Inspecting more of them confirms the same thing without adding new playbook value.

## What each file contains
For every primary page on each site:
- URL
- Desktop + mobile above-fold composition
- Primary CTA
- Density, trust signals, lead-capture friction
- Free-vs-gated boundary
- Specific moves worth stealing
- Specific moves worth avoiding
- One-line verdict

Plus a cross-page pattern read and a "what Ken Research should beat them at" section per file.

## How this feeds back into the workspace
Once the deep-dives land, the synthesis (this folder's [`_synthesis.md`](_synthesis.md), TBD) will pull the cross-cutting patterns and update [`../../04-experience-playbook/`](../../04-experience-playbook/) so each surface playbook references specific competitor pages by URL — not just "be design-led."
