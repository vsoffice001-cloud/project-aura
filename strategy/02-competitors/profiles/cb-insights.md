# CB Insights — Competitor Profile

**Last updated:** April 2026  
**Tier:** C — Experience-led / data-platform competitor  
**Website:** cbinsights.com

---

## One-Line Positioning

**In their own words:** "Predictive intelligence on private companies" — helping strategy and deal teams "see opportunity before the market does."  
**Plain language:** New York-based intelligence platform that has evolved from a venture data tracker into an AI-powered strategy terminal for corporate development, M&A, and innovation teams. The most product-forward research company in the competitive set.

## Sectors / Verticals Covered

Cross-industry focus on private company intelligence: software, cloud, banking/fintech, pharma, telecom. Specializes in coverage of emerging technology markets and startups. Not a traditional market sizing research firm — focuses on company-level intelligence.

## Digital Experience Notes

### Homepage
- **The most distinct homepage in the entire competitive set.** CB Insights' homepage reads like a software company, not a research firm.
- Primary framing: "See opportunity before the market does" — outcome-first, not data-first.
- Tab-based workflow illustration: "Strategize → Longlist → Shortlist → Engage → Monitor" — describes the buyer's job-to-be-done workflow, not the product features.
- Quantified proof of outcomes: "2.8x more acquisitions," "70% faster revenue growth" — specific business impact claims.
- Enterprise logo carousel: "10 largest businesses," "All Big 4," "86% of software companies" — premium client positioning.
- Large CTA hierarchy: "Start free trial" + "Contact sales" — freemium entry point is clearly visible.
- Visual design: modern, dark backgrounds, professional typography, data visualization previews. Among the most polished in the entire competitive set.

### Strategy Terminal (Platform)
- Web app for company discovery, analysis, and portfolio tracking.
- **11M+ company profiles** in the database.
- **1,600+ technology market rankings** — categorized by sector and sub-sector.
- **Proprietary signals:** Mosaic Score (company health), commercial maturity assessment, exit probability modeling — genuinely predictive, not just descriptive.
- AI Agents: automated company scouting, due diligence report generation, M&A target identification.
- ChatCBI: conversational strategy AI with access to CB Insights' proprietary data.
- Integrations: Salesforce, Snowflake, Microsoft 365 Copilot — enterprise workflow embedding.
- Visual intelligence: strategy maps, relationship graphs, market maps.

### Research Reports (Editorial)
- Free editorial research at cbinsights.com/research — no paywall on research browsing.
- Reports accessible via email gate (not subscription wall).
- Card-based report tiles with thumbnail imagery — visual, scannable.
- Filtering by technology topic and industry — cleaner than most competitors' catalog UX.
- Webinar content: "Register Now" / "Get the recording" for past events.
- Research quality is genuinely high — data-backed, visually polished, editorially voiced.
- Chart previews embedded directly on research hub pages — previews available before clicking through.
- Free entry point with newsletter/email capture — content marketing funnel is well-designed.

### Lead-Capture Flow
- **Least aggressive lead capture in the competitive set.**
- Free trial (10 days) available without sales call.
- Email gate on research downloads (not form with phone/company/title requirements).
- Newsletter signup as softest entry point.
- The freemium mechanic drives qualified leads rather than volume leads.

### Mobile Responsiveness
- Website appears responsive and well-designed on mobile.
- Platform terminal is primarily a desktop/laptop experience by nature of the data density.
- No dedicated native app found.

### Loading Speed (subjective)
- Fast. Modern frontend architecture, well-optimized assets.

### Search / Discoverability
- ChatCBI and AI Agents provide conversational discovery within the platform.
- Public research hub has category filtering — above average for the category.
- SEO generates traffic through free editorial research content.

## Standout Strength

- **Workflow-first UX thinking** — "Strategize → Longlist → Shortlist → Engage → Monitor" is the clearest articulation of buyer workflow seen across the entire competitive set. The product is designed around what buyers do, not what the firm produces.
- **Freemium + free trial model** — the least friction entry in Tier C.
- **AI agents as core product** — not a marketing add-on but the primary product evolution direction. CB Insights is the most AI-native in terms of product experience.
- **Research hub is genuinely useful and free** — drives organic traffic, builds brand authority, provides sales qualification without friction.
- **Mosaic Score and predictive signals** — proprietary AI-derived signals create genuine information asymmetry for subscribers.

---

## Visible Weakness

- **Significant headcount reduction** from ~380+ (2022) to ~263 (late 2023) — raises questions about platform investment velocity.
- **Revenue estimate** (~$79.8M) is relatively modest for the platform ambition — may limit R&D investment.
- **Company-intelligence focus** doesn't directly compete with market sizing/syndicated reports — different buyer persona.
- **Pricing opacity** despite freemium positioning — no pricing page visible.
- **ChatCBI and AI Agents** are differentiated claims but unverifiable quality without access.

---

## What Ken Research Should Steal / Avoid

- **Steal:** Workflow-first product framing — "Strategize → Longlist → Shortlist → Engage → Monitor" is a masterclass in UX positioning. Ken Research should map its products to buyer job stages, not to internal production categories.
- **Steal:** Free editorial research as a top-of-funnel content layer — not gated, not form-heavy, just genuinely useful content that builds trust before the sale.
- **Steal:** The 10-day free trial concept — some form of self-service preview is essential for any platform product.
- **Avoid:** The company-level intelligence focus is not directly Ken Research's lane — but the UX approach is fully transferable to market intelligence contexts.
