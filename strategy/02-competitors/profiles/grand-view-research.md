## Standout Strength

- **Most explicitly productized portfolio** in Tier A — Horizon, Brainshare, Pipeline, and Signal are named products, not just service descriptions. This signals stronger brand architecture.
- **Free sample download as primary CTA** — lower friction than inquiry-only peers; more conversion-friendly.
- **San Francisco HQ** (even if operations are India-based) — useful perception in enterprise sales.
- **150+ consulting projects monthly** claim suggests genuine consulting scale.

---

## Visible Weakness

- **Platform UX (Horizon) is largely invisible** to potential buyers — no public preview, no screenshots, no interactive demo.
- **Design doesn't match the product sophistication** — the website looks like a mid-tier Indian research firm despite the more sophisticated product portfolio.
- **No pricing transparency** on any product.
- **Catalog at 10,000+ reports** has navigation depth issues — hard to discover without already knowing what you're looking for.

---

## What Ken Research Should Steal / Avoid

- **Steal:** Named product architecture (Horizon, Brainshare, Signal). Named products feel more premium than undifferentiated "reports" and "consulting" — gives buyers something to evaluate and remember.
- **Steal:** Making "free sample" the primary CTA rather than "request a quote" — meaningfully lowers conversion friction.
- **Avoid:** Keeping the platform experience entirely invisible to prospects. If Ken Research develops any proprietary analytical product, showing it (even partially) is a major competitive advantage.
