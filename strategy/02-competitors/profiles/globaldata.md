**Tier:** B — Premium / consulting-flavored competitor

**In their own words:** "Better Business Decisions at the Speed of AI" / "The future of intelligence"

## Digital Experience Notes

### Homepage
- "Better Business Decisions at the Speed of AI" — strong, clean positioning statement.
- "Meet Ava" CTA is prominent on the homepage — AI assistant as the primary product differentiator.
- Enterprise logo carousel: Nestlé, Deloitte, AstraZeneca, Vodafone — genuinely premium client base.
- "DECODED" newsletter signup integrated throughout — content marketing layer.
- Modular explanation of platform features (numbered 1-5) — clearer feature communication than most peers.
- Sticky navigation with collapsible menus.
- 2,000 in-house subject matter experts prominently stated.
- Visual design: polished, dark/navy palette with clean typography. Above average for the category; feels like a legitimate platform company, not a report-seller.

### Platform / Intelligence Centers
- Platform described as configurable with: Strategic Intelligence, Sales Intelligence, Market Intelligence, Competitive Intelligence, Deals Intelligence, Innovation Intelligence — six distinct workflow modules.
- **Ava AI:** Research analyst AI tool that answers strategic questions from the platform's data.
- Industry Intelligence Centers: dedicated portals per sector (e.g., Healthcare Intelligence, Technology Intelligence) — not just a general report store.
- Custom solutions developed with in-house consultants.
- API & Data Feeds for system integration.

### Data & Insights Section
- 28M+ data points across 22 industries.
- Advanced search feature with refinement options.
- Browsable by "Insight by Sector" (22 industries) or "Insight Type" (company performance, macroeconomic, market sizing, ESG).
- Thematic categories: AI, Cloud, Cybersecurity, ESG.
- "Hot Topics" and "Signals" sections — trend-surfacing features.
- ESG-specific resources with subsections for Environment, Social, Governance.

### Lead-Capture Flow
- Demo-first model. No self-service access.
- "DECODED" newsletter as a lower-friction entry point.
- Multiple CTAs directing to demo booking.

### Mobile Responsiveness
- Site appears responsive; enterprise users likely primarily desktop.
- No dedicated mobile app found.

### Loading Speed (subjective)
- Fast on homepage and product pages. Dynamic platform elements likely have variable loading.

### Search / Discoverability
- Advanced search on data/insights section.
- Ava AI enables conversational query for subscribers.
- Public site navigation is hierarchical by industry and workflow type.

## Standout Strength

- **Public company with disclosed financials** — unusual in the category; creates legitimacy and transparency.
- **74% subscription revenue** signals strong recurring relationships and product stickiness.
- **Ava AI** — the most developed AI assistant positioning in the competitive set outside Euromonitor Passport AI.
- **Six distinct workflow modules** (Strategic, Sales, Market, Competitive, Deals, Innovation Intelligence) — the most sophisticated product architecture in the competitive set.
- **3,500+ employees** — genuine research depth across 24 industries.
- **DECODED newsletter** — content-first approach to enterprise audience building.

---

## Visible Weakness

- **Organic revenue growth of +1%** in 2025 (rest was acquisition-driven) — indicates platform growth is slowing.
- **Dropped £500M revenue target for end-2026** — suggests growth ambitions are being revised downward.
- **Integration of acquisitions is a drag** on platform coherence — breadth from M&A can mean inconsistent user experience across different Intelligence Centers.
- **No self-service or freemium path** — the entire funnel is enterprise sales.
- **Pricing entirely opaque** — no signals to prospective buyers on value-to-cost.

---

## What Ken Research Should Steal / Avoid

- **Steal:** The six-workflow-module platform architecture — naming workflows by buyer job (Strategic Intelligence, Sales Intelligence, etc.) is a much stronger UX and sales framing than "research reports."
- **Steal:** Ava-style AI assistant as a platform feature — the "ask a question, get an answer with sources" UX is where research delivery is heading.
- **Steal:** DECODED newsletter as an audience-building mechanism — episodic editorial content positioned as intelligence, not marketing.
- **Avoid:** Acquisition-led growth that creates platform fragmentation. Ken Research should build depth and coherence in fewer verticals rather than buying breadth.
