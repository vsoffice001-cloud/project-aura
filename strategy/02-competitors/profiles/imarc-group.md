## Digital Experience Notes

### Homepage
- Standard mega-menu navigation with 14 industry dropdowns.
- Client logo carousel (Microsoft, FedEx, Maersk, UPS) as social proof.
- Video testimonial section — relatively unusual in the category.
- "IMARC Engineering" banner promoting a new service line; feels bolted on, clutters the hierarchy.
- Hero copy is generic ("Market Research Company, Reports and Consulting Services") — no differentiated brand voice.
- Color palette: corporate blues and whites, nothing memorable.

### Report Catalog / Store UX
- URL: `imarcgroup.com/reports`
- Basic search bar labeled "Search Reports."
- Industry filter as top-level nav only — no multi-faceted filtering (price, date, format, etc.) visible.
- No pricing shown in catalog cards.
- "Inquire Before Buying" model creates friction for self-service buyers.
- 404 errors found on some report URLs tested, suggesting catalog maintenance gaps.

### Sample Preview UX
- Sample requests require form fill (name, email minimum).
- No visible page-count or chapter preview before requesting.
- Sample delivery likely email-based rather than instant download.

### Checkout / Lead-Capture Flow
- Heavy reliance on inquiry/contact forms rather than e-commerce checkout.
- Multiple CTAs: "Request a Sample," "Inquire Before Buying," "Speak to Analyst."
- No self-service purchase visible.

### Dashboards / Platform
- `360.imarcgroup.com` portal exists but is login-gated; no public preview of the interface.
- [Unverified] assumed to be a basic report-access portal rather than an interactive analytics dashboard.

### Mobile Responsiveness
- Appears responsive at page level; mega-menus likely problematic on small screens.
- No dedicated mobile app found.

### Loading Speed (subjective)
- Moderate. Standard shared CMS hosting feel. No noticeable lag on homepage, but deep catalog pages load slowly.

### Search / Discoverability
- Basic keyword search only.
- No AI search, no faceted filtering, no tag browsing visible.
- Discovery relies on navigation hierarchy rather than search-first.

## Standout Strength

- **Breadth of catalog:** 15,000+ reports across 14 verticals is genuinely comprehensive.
- **Feasibility / EPCM consulting:** Adding engineering services differentiates from pure-play research firms.
- **Client referral rate:** Claims 65% of new business from referrals — suggests strong delivery quality even if the website doesn't reflect it.

---

## Visible Weakness

- **Design maturity is low.** The homepage looks like a 2016-era corporate website with clip-art icons and stock photography.
- **No pricing transparency.** Fully inquiry-driven in an era when buyers expect self-serve options.
- **No real data platform or dashboard.** The `360.imarcgroup.com` portal is opaque.
- **Catalog UX is a basic list.** Filtering, comparison, and preview capabilities are minimal.
- **Engineering division announcement feels bolted on** — dilutes the research brand rather than elevating it.

---

## What Ken Research Should Steal / Avoid

- **Steal:** Video testimonials (rare in the category; adds authenticity). Breadth-of-coverage messaging for specific verticals where Ken has depth.
- **Avoid:** The inquiry-only purchasing model. In 2026, buyers expect to at minimum see pricing before raising their hand. IMARC's opacity frustrates self-serve discovery.
