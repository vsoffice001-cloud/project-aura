## Digital Experience Notes

### Homepage
- "Trusted Tech Intelligence" — clean, authoritative positioning.
- "IDC Quanta" AI platform launch prominently bannered — positions IDC as actively evolving.
- Key trust metrics: "1,300 experts," "200+ research programs," "11B+ ICT datapoints."
- Five consecutive "Analyst Firm of the Year" badges (2020-2024) — significant credibility signal.
- Multiple event CTAs (benchmark brief, upcoming events, webinars) — events are a meaningful conversion and relationship tool.
- Hero messaging emphasizes "global and local analysis."
- Visual design: clean, professional, authoritative. IDC's visual identity feels corporate but trustworthy — consistent with enterprise technology buyer expectations.
- The homepage redirects authenticated users to my.idc.com — different experiences for prospects vs. subscribers.

### Research / Platform UX
- **MyIDC:** Subscription portal accessible at my.idc.com. [403 on direct access — subscribers only.]
- **IDC Quanta:** New AI platform — positioning unclear from public site; likely an AI layer on top of the existing data infrastructure.
- **Black Book:** Structured spending data product for technology budget benchmarking.
- **Tracker®:** Vendor market share tracking products (server market, PC market, etc.) — most cited IDC content type in industry press.
- **Spending Guide:** IT budget forecast products.
- **Wallet:** Channel partner ecosystem intelligence.
- Research format: mostly gated research documents (PDFs) plus interactive data tools for subscribers.

### Individual Research Pages
- Site redirects to my.idc.com for research access — authentication wall even for free press releases.
- Redirect behavior (302 to my.idc.com) means all deep IDC content is subscriber-gated.
- This is the most aggressively gated experience in the competitive set — very little free content.

### Events UX
- CIO Summit, IT Security Summit, AI & Data Summit, FutureTech Summit — multiple named executive events.
- Events appear to be premium-access, invitation-type gatherings rather than mass conferences.

### Mobile Responsiveness
- Main site responsive; my.idc.com portal likely primarily desktop experience.
- No dedicated mobile app found.

### Loading Speed (subjective)
- Fast on main marketing pages. Portal performance unknown.

### Search / Discoverability
- Heavy SEO authority — IDC press releases and Trackers are widely cited in tech media.
- Public search limited; most research content is subscriber-only.
- IDC Quanta positioning suggests AI-powered research discovery is coming or in beta.

## Standout Strength

- **"Analyst Firm of the Year" five years running** — institutional credibility that is nearly impossible to compete with directly.
- **Tracker products** (server market share, PC market, mobile) are the de facto industry standard that all tech vendors use — creates enormous organic citation and brand building.
- **11B+ ICT data points** — the deepest technology market data repository in the category.
- **Events as enterprise relationship building** — named executive summits create access that report-only competitors cannot.
- **IDC Quanta** — early positioning as an AI-native intelligence platform signals awareness of where the market is heading.

---

## Visible Weakness

- **Most aggressively gated** experience in the set — even press releases redirect to authentication. This limits organic discovery.
- **Technology-only focus** limits relevance to non-tech vertical research buyers.
- **Visual design is functional but dated** — no meaningful design investment visible beyond competent corporate standard.
- **Revenue opacity** — despite scale, no public financials due to private ownership.
- **IDC Quanta** is vaporware-adjacent in public messaging — no public demo, no screenshots, no use cases on the main site.

---

## What Ken Research Should Steal / Avoid

- **Steal:** Named proprietary dataset products (Tracker, Black Book, Spending Guide) — IDC's Tracker reports are cited universally in tech media because they contain original, authoritative data that no one else produces. Ken Research should identify 2-3 verticals where it could produce a definitive named dataset.
- **Steal:** Event-linked research distribution — executive summits that debut new research create earned media and relationship capital simultaneously.
- **Avoid:** The authentication wall for all content. IDC can do this because its brand is so established that buyers seek it out. For Ken Research, free visible content is a necessary top-of-funnel investment.
