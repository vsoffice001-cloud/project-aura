# MarketsandMarkets — Competitor Profile

**Last updated:** April 2026  
**Tier:** A — Direct syndicated-report competitor

## One-Line Positioning

**In their own words:** "Revenue Impact & Advisory Company"  
**Plain language:** India-based research firm that has repositioned aggressively from "reports publisher" to "strategic intelligence platform," with AI-powered products (KnowledgeStore, GrowthIQ, SalesPlay) sitting alongside its core syndicated report catalog.

## Pricing Visible on Site

**No.** No pricing is displayed publicly. KnowledgeStore and GrowthIQ are demo-first products. Reports also require contact/inquiry. Individual report listing pages show "$4,950" on some listing card summaries, suggesting per-report pricing exists, but the path to purchase is inquiry-first.

## Digital Experience Notes

### Homepage
- The most "platform company" positioned homepage in Tier A.
- Messaging has shifted heavily toward AI ("AI Economy," "KnowledgeStore," "GrowthIQ") — the homepage reads more like a SaaS product than a research firm.
- Multi-level mega-menu navigation covering industry, practice area, and megatrend.
- Carousel highlights AI-focused offerings prominently.
- "$140 Bn revenue impact delivered to clients" claim is bold but creates credibility questions without supporting evidence.
- 37+ NPS score displayed — one of few research firms to surface NPS as a trust signal.
- Design: dark navy headers, clean typography. More polished than most Tier A peers, though still feels 2018-era SaaS rather than 2026-era.

### Report Catalog / Store UX
- Large catalog navigated through industry/practice mega-menu.
- Some listing pages show "$4,950" per-report pricing in catalog cards — partially transparent.
- "TOC Available" notation on listing cards — signals preview availability.
- No faceted filtering (format, date, region) visible.

### Platform UX — KnowledgeStore
- Described as "one-click interactive dashboard" for high-growth market exploration.
- Four capability pillars: Predictive Intelligence, Opportunity Mapping, Signal Detection, Decision Acceleration.
- Covers 30,000+ high-growth opportunities across 13+ industries.
- 650 analysts supporting the platform.
- No public demo of the actual interface; all conversion via "Schedule a Free Demo."
- [Unverified] Based on descriptions, appears to be a structured data portal with analytics overlays rather than a true interactive dashboard in the Tableau/Power BI sense.

### Platform UX — GrowthIQ
- AI-powered, generates "15+ consulting-grade strategy documents" instantly.
- Board-ready output positioning — targets strategy executives, not just researchers.
- Also requires demo/contact for access.

### Individual Report Page UX
- Standard industry pattern: TOC tab, sample download form, "Buy Now" button.
- No pricing displayed on individual pages without requesting a sample.
- Data visualizations described in listings but not previewed on the report page itself.

### Checkout / Lead-Capture Flow
- Entirely inquiry-based. No e-commerce checkout.
- 404 error on attempted PDF download link (observed during research) — catalog maintenance concern.
- Multiple CTAs: "Speak to Analyst," "Download Sample," "Request Demo," "Get Quote."

### Mobile Responsiveness
- Appears responsive. Platform products likely not optimized for mobile.
- No dedicated mobile app found.

### Loading Speed (subjective)
- Moderate. Some pages load slowly; likely shared hosting for catalog pages.

### Search / Discoverability
- Basic keyword search; no AI search visible on catalog.
- GrowthIQ and KnowledgeStore likely have internal search for subscribers but not publicly accessible.

## Standout Strength

- **Most aggressive product evolution** in Tier A — the KnowledgeStore + GrowthIQ + SalesPlay stack represents a genuine attempt to become a platform company rather than a report publisher.
- **NPS signaling** — displaying a 37 NPS is an unusually metric-driven trust approach.
- **Scale** — ~1,661 employees and 650+ analysts gives them genuine research depth.
- **"Revenue Impact" positioning** ties deliverables to business outcomes rather than just data delivery.

---

## Visible Weakness

- **The platform ambitions outpace the visible UX.** KnowledgeStore and GrowthIQ are hidden behind demo walls; buyers can't self-evaluate.
- **No pricing transparency** anywhere — not on reports, not on platforms.
- **Credibility gap on claims.** "$140 Bn revenue impact" and "200,000+ connected markets" are impressive-sounding but unverifiable.
- **404 errors** on some pages suggest catalog management issues at scale.
- **Visual design hasn't caught up** with the SaaS positioning — feels like a 2018 report-seller trying to be a 2026 platform company.

---

## What Ken Research Should Steal / Avoid

- **Steal:** The "Revenue Impact" positioning frame — tying research to client business outcomes is a more compelling value proposition than "access to reports."
- **Steal:** The NPS display as a trust signal; it is unusual and differentiating in the category.
- **Avoid:** Hiding the platform experience behind demo walls. If Ken Research builds any kind of intelligence platform, a public interactive demo or a limited free tier will outperform a form-gated demo every time.
