## Digital Experience Notes

### Report Side (statista.com standard)

#### Homepage
- Clean, modern, search-first layout — the most consumer-friendly homepage in the category.
- Prominent search bar with search examples that educate new users.
- Daily trending statistics carousel adds freshness and reason to visit regularly.
- "Insights and facts across 170 industries and 150+ countries" is a clear, credible value claim.
- Navigation organized by industry, function, and use case — multiple entry paths.
- API/MCP integrations prominently mentioned — signals developer and enterprise-first thinking.
- AI research assistants (ChatGPT-style) mentioned as product offering.
- Visual design: clean, white, modern. The most polished homepage in the entire competitive set.

#### Statistic/Data Pages
- Individual statistic pages show chart preview with key figures visible.
- **Paywall model:** numbers are obscured (shown as "***") for non-subscribers; a clear preview of the chart trend is visible.
- The paywall mechanic is well-designed — you can see the shape of the data and understand its relevance before subscribing.
- "Visit original statistic" links enable deeper exploration without losing context.
- Related statistics section (20+ suggestions) — strong cross-sell and content discovery.
- Download formats: PNG, PDF, XLS, PPT — available to subscribers.
- **Accessibility note:** Charts are flagged as "not accessible to screen readers" — a real gap in 2026.
- Context paragraphs ("AI demands data," "AI could boost productivity growth") add editorial value alongside raw data.

#### Lead-Capture/Paywall UX
- Free tier exists — users can access limited data without credit card.
- Freemium conversion model is the most buyer-friendly in the category.
- "Register for free" CTAs alongside download buttons.
- No aggressive modal popups observed.

### Dashboard Product (Statista Platform Tier)

#### Dashboard Features
- Consumer Insights interactive tool — custom surveys and consumer data.
- Infographic creation tools for data storytelling.
- Market research automation for enterprise teams.
- API access (Rest API, MCP integrations) for embedding data in other platforms.
- askStatista custom research on demand.

### Search / Discoverability
- **Excellent.** Search is the primary entry mechanism with prominent placement and query examples.
- Search indexes across all 1M+ statistics — genuinely useful for finding specific data points.
- Category navigation available as secondary entry.
- AI research assistants add a conversational search layer.

## Standout Strength

- **Pricing transparency** — full public pricing tiers is a category-defining differentiator. Buyers can self-evaluate and self-serve.
- **Consumer-grade UX** — the most accessible, modern visual design in the entire 20-company set. Statista looks like a product company, not a report publisher.
- **Freemium model** — the free tier drives enormous organic traffic and enables buyer self-qualification before sale.
- **API/MCP integrations** — developer-friendly access points that no Tier A peer offers.
- **Breadth** — 1M+ statistics across 170 industries at known price points is a fundamentally different value proposition from $5K-per-report peers.
- **Trending statistics feed** gives a daily reason to visit — drives habit and retention.

---

## Visible Weakness

- **Depth vs. breadth trade-off.** Statista is famously wide but shallow — individual statistics lack the contextual analysis that a $5K report from Mordor or MarketsandMarkets provides.
- **Chart accessibility gap** — flagging charts as not accessible to screen readers is a meaningful oversight in 2026.
- **Dynamic paywall** (numbers shown as "***") can feel manipulative once users understand the pattern.
- **German company / Hamburg HQ** — some perception bias from US enterprise buyers who prefer American platforms.
- **askStatista custom research** is an add-on, not a core strength — positioning as a "strategy consulting" firm at the lower tier is a stretch.

---

## What Ken Research Should Steal / Avoid

- **Steal:** Full public pricing transparency. Statista proves that publishing your prices builds trust and drives conversion — it doesn't commoditize you.
- **Steal:** Freemium entry — even a "view 3 reports per month free" tier would dramatically change Ken Research's inbound funnel.
- **Steal:** The trending/fresh content feed on the homepage — it creates daily visit reasons and signals an active, current research capability.
- **Avoid:** Being as shallow as Statista on depth. Ken Research's depth per vertical is a competitive advantage against Statista's breadth model.
