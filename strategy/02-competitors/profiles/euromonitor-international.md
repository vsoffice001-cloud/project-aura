## Digital Experience Notes

### Homepage
- "First destination for organisations seeking growth" — strong positioning claim.
- "Global market intelligence to help you make high-stakes decisions with confidence" — outcome-focused framing, not just data delivery.
- 15 clearly defined industry verticals with strong visual presentation.
- Scale claims are specific and credible: 210 countries, 16 offices, 100+ country analysts, 65,000 users.
- Passport AI is prominently featured as an innovation differentiator.
- Visual design: one of the more professionally designed homepages in the category. Clean, structured, with good use of data-forward imagery.
- No pricing — full enterprise sales model.

### Passport Platform
- **Three-layer architecture:** Strategic knowledge (long-term decisions) + Tactical data (quarterly/annual tracking) + Actionable insights (expert analysis).
- Dashboard features: Visual dashboards for trend spotting, Forecast models for scenario testing, Data manipulation tools for custom datasets.
- **Passport AI:** Natural language queries, concise multi-source summaries, uncovers "hidden connections and trends" beyond keyword search.
- API integration for workflow embedding.
- Data exports: Excel, PDF, PowerPoint.
- Covers: category market sizes, company/brand data, historic information, and forecasts across 210 countries.

### Passport Platform UX (based on user reviews and G2 data)
- **Strengths:** Data is in "neat tabular form readily shared with clients"; getting reports is described as "very easy"; interactive world map dashboard is distinctive.
- **Weaknesses:** Performance lags when adding multiple geographies and industry segments simultaneously; some users report slow download speeds for complex datasets.
- G2 reviews available at g2.com — social proof layer that most peers lack.

### Report Store UX
- "Tens of thousands of downloadable annual reports."
- Standard catalog browsing by industry.
- [Unverified] Pricing not publicly shown; likely contact-based for non-subscribers.

### Mobile Responsiveness
- Appears responsive; enterprise users likely primarily desktop.
- No dedicated mobile app found.

### Loading Speed (subjective)
- Fast on marketing pages. Passport platform performance varies by query complexity per user reviews.

### Search / Discoverability
- Passport AI enables natural language search — the most sophisticated search capability in the competitive set for subscribed users.
- Public site uses standard keyword search.

## Standout Strength

- **Passport platform is the most mature named research platform** in the competitive set — 65,000+ users, 5,700+ organizations, 50+ years of data is a genuine moat.
- **210-country coverage** with on-the-ground analysts is unmatched by any Tier A peer.
- **Passport AI** — natural language query capability is genuinely ahead of most peers who are still marketing "AI-powered" but mean "keyword search with ML ranking."
- **Founded 1972** — institutional longevity creates trust signals that Indian-origin firms cannot replicate quickly.
- **G2 social proof** — having reviews on a neutral platform (G2) provides third-party credibility.
- **Consumer goods depth** — if you work in FMCG, Passport is considered the category standard.

---

## Visible Weakness

- **No pricing transparency** anywhere.
- **Consumer/FMCG heavy** — less relevant for technology, SaaS, B2B services sectors. Ken Research may have vertical advantages.
- **Performance lag** at high complexity queries — a platform UX issue that impacts power users.
- **Enterprise-only model** — no freemium or self-service path limits top-of-funnel reach.
- **Outdated feel in some secondary pages** — the core Passport marketing is polished, but some deeper site sections show their age.

---

## What Ken Research Should Steal / Avoid

- **Steal:** Three-layer platform architecture framing (strategic knowledge + tactical data + actionable insights) — this is a clear, buyer-comprehensible way to explain a complex product.
- **Steal:** Natural language AI search as a research entry point — this is the future direction for any research platform UX.
- **Steal:** G2 reviews as a trust layer — getting on neutral review platforms provides credibility that self-published testimonials cannot.
- **Avoid:** The pure enterprise-only, no-pricing model — it limits discoverability and top-of-funnel. Euromonitor can afford this because of 50+ years of brand equity; Ken Research cannot.
