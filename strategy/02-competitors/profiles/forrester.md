# Forrester Research — Competitor Profile

**Last updated:** April 2026  
**Tier:** B — Premium / consulting-flavored competitor  
**Website:** forrester.com

---

## One-Line Positioning

**In their own words:** "Be Bold" (brand tagline); core positioning: "Empower bold, decisive action through research and advisory"  
**Plain language:** Cambridge, MA-based publicly traded research firm focused on technology, business strategy, and customer experience — Gartner's closest peer brand, but with a stronger B2C/marketing lens and a distinguishable AI research layer.

## Digital Experience Notes

### Homepage
- Clean, professional design with strong editorial voice.
- Multi-path navigation: "Who We Serve" (audience role), "What We Offer" (products), "How We Think" (editorial topics) — role-first IA similar to Gartner, though slightly less developed.
- Emphasis on "decisive action" over data delivery — same outcome-positioning as Gartner.
- "Forrester AI" — an AI research assistant — prominently featured as a product feature.
- "Forrester Wave™" — proprietary competitive analysis framework (analogous to Gartner Magic Quadrant).
- "Our Objectivity" section reinforces editorial independence — important trust signal.
- ESG, NPS, and leadership profiles add institutional credibility.
- Complimentary Webinars and Analyst Briefings as low-friction entry points — unusual transparency from a subscription firm.
- Visual design: polished, typographically strong, use of white space is above category average. "Be Bold" brand campaign gives it a distinctive voice that most research firms lack.

### Research Platform UX
- **Forrester Decisions:** Role-specific guidance subscriptions (for CIOs, CMOs, security leaders, etc.) — structured by job function, not industry.
- **Forrester Market Insights:** Competitive landscape reports analogous to Magic Quadrant.
- **Forrester Wave™:** Proprietary vendor evaluation framework — same mechanic as Gartner's MQ, with a line graph format distinguishing it visually from the 2x2 quadrant.
- **Forrester AI:** AI research assistant integrated into the subscription platform — conversational research query capability.
- Research emphasis: "actionable guidance" and "catalysts for decisive organizational action" — positioning research as consulting-grade insight, not just information access.

### Hybrid Freemium Elements
- Free webinars and analyst briefings reduce barrier to first engagement.
- Some reports available on gated (email-only) basis.
- Forrester.com blogs and thought leadership are freely accessible.
- "Become a Client" wall for subscription research.

### Individual Research Pages
- Forrester Wave reports: available as gated downloads (email form) — accessible without subscription.
- Research pages emphasize frameworks and key findings over raw data.
- Visual design on research deliverables is consistently high quality — branded chart templates, consistent typography.

### Mobile Responsiveness
- Site appears responsive; research portal primarily desktop experience.
- No dedicated mobile app found.

### Loading Speed (subjective)
- Fast. Clean site architecture, few heavy assets on marketing pages.

### Search / Discoverability
- Strong SEO presence for enterprise technology and marketing strategy queries.
- Forrester Wave reports generate significant inbound SEO traffic.
- AI-assisted research discovery within subscription platform.

## Standout Strength

- **"Be Bold" brand positioning** — one of the few research firms with a distinctive editorial voice. "Be Bold" is a marketing brand campaign, not just a tagline — it shows investment in brand beyond product features.
- **Forrester Wave™ visual format** — line graph format distinguishes it from Gartner's 2x2 and creates proprietary visual identity in research deliverables.
- **Forrester AI** as a native platform feature — conversational research access is the right UX direction.
- **Free webinars and briefings** — lower-friction entry points than peers.
- **Editorial objectivity emphasis** — "Our Objectivity" as a site section signals awareness that methodology credibility is a competitive differentiator.

---

## Visible Weakness

- **Revenue declining** — -10% in 2024, -8.7% TTM in 2025. This is a concerning trend for a publicly traded firm and likely results in reduced investment capacity.
- **Smaller than Gartner by 5x** in revenue, 10x in employees — cannot match Gartner's research depth or brand ubiquity.
- **Technology and marketing focus limits** relevance for industrial, chemicals, healthcare market sizing buyers — less relevant to Ken Research's core verticals.
- **No freemium product** — the Peer Insights equivalent (open review platform) is weaker than Gartner's.
- **Revenue decline** may lead to content quality/volume reductions over time.

---

## What Ken Research Should Steal / Avoid

- **Steal:** "Be Bold" style brand positioning — research firms can have editorial voice and brand personality without sacrificing objectivity. Ken Research needs a strong point of view.
- **Steal:** Forrester Wave line-graph format concept — Ken Research should develop a proprietary visual format for competitive analysis deliverables that becomes recognizable.
- **Steal:** Free analyst briefings as a low-friction entry point — inviting prospects into a conversation with analysts before asking for a subscription is a genuinely different approach.
- **Avoid:** Revenue-declining patterns — Forrester's decline seems tied to subscription model friction and competition from AI-powered alternatives. Research firms need to add genuine analytical value that AI tools can't replicate, not just aggregate data.
