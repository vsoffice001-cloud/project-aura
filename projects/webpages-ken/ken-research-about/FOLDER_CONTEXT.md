# Ken Research — About Us Page

**What**: Static HTML/CSS/JS About Us page for Ken Research.
**Why**: Standalone cinematic page showcasing the Ken Research team and company history.
**Stack**: Vanilla HTML, CSS, JavaScript (no build step)
**Theme**: Dark cinematic with GSAP scroll animations
**Key Files**:
- `index.html` — Main page structure
- `style.css` — All styles
- `main.js` — GSAP animations and interactivity
- `*.png` — Team member photos (Ankur, Avdhesh, Namit)

**Run**: Open `index.html` directly in a browser, or use a local server.
**Skills to use**: `frontend-design`, `gsap-core`, `gsap-scrolltrigger`
