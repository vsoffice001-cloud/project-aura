## Cross-cutting issues

### Bg alternation pattern
All §01–§21 sections sit inside the white `bg-[var(--color-foundation-white)]` wrapper — no bg alternation within the SideTOC body. This is intentional for this PDP design (continuous editorial reading). The alternation happens at the macro level:
- Hero: cinematic dark
- Body §01–§21: editorial white (flat)
- §22: toggleable (white default / dark on user action)
- §23: editorial white
- §24: cinematic dark

No bug — this matches the recipe intent for a research PDP (continuous reading > chunked alternation).
