# Voice — Research Pillar

**Color tag:** purple `#806ce0` accent on editorial light, Ken Red `#b01f24` for CTAs only  
**Lives on:** Report Store, listing pages, report detail, sector pages, market trackers, industry spotlights, recommended-for-you sections

---

## Identity

The librarian's voice. Precise, neutral, inventory-led. Helps the reader find, scan, decide. Earns trust through specificity, not adjectives.

---

## When to use this surface

- Report Store homepage + listing pages
- Report detail pages
- Industry / sector landing pages
- Market trackers ("CAGR Leaderboard", "Market Entry Hotspots")
- Recommended-for-you / Trending / Top Downloads sections
- Search result pages
- Filter / browse interfaces

**NOT for:** case study narratives (use Consulting voice), survey product pages (use Surveys voice), DS docs (use Foundations voice).

---

## Tone

**3 adjectives:** Precise · Neutral · Scannable.  
**1 forbidden adjective:** Hyperbolic.

The Research reader is in scan-mode: filter → preview → decide. They reject promotional copy, require structured data, and need every label to mean something. Voice serves discovery, not persuasion.

---

## Vocabulary

**Allowed (use these):**
report · methodology · sector · vertical · industry · region · CAGR · market size · forecast · segment · sample · respondent · published · refreshed · coverage · scope · executive summary · table of contents · download · preview · sample · indicator · benchmark · breakdown · data point · data set · analyst · research note

**Forbidden (avoid):**
cutting-edge · game-changing · revolutionary · best-in-class · world-class · ultimate guide · everything you need to know · ground-breaking · paradigm shift · must-read · top-tier · #1 · the only · elite

---

## Sentence patterns

**GOOD:**
> India FMCG Market Report 2026 — sector breakdown, CAGR projection, and 15-segment outlook.

**BAD:**
> The ultimate guide to India's revolutionary FMCG market — everything you need to know!

**GOOD:**
> 200+ pages · 18 charts · 12 segments · Published Q1 2026.

**BAD:**
> A massive, comprehensive, must-read report packed with insights!

**GOOD:**
> Methodology: Top-down market sizing combined with primary survey of 412 respondents across 6 cities.

**BAD:**
> Our world-class methodology delivers cutting-edge insights you can trust.

**GOOD:**
> CAGR 8.4% (2024–2029).

**BAD:**
> Explosive growth at an incredible 8.4% CAGR!

---

## Reading level target

Flesch-Kincaid Grade 9-11. Information-dense but accessible. Short sentences. Active voice. Strong nouns.

Most copy is **fragments + structured metadata**, not flowing prose. Reports = data products, not editorial.

---

## Headline rules

- **Page h1 (sector / category landing):** 3-6 words, descriptive. *"India Healthcare Reports"* · *"GCC Market Coverage"* · *"FMCG Sector Intelligence"*.
- **Report card title:** Full descriptive title from publication. *"India Pharmaceutical Market Outlook 2026"* — never abbreviated, never punchy.
- **Section h2 (Trending, Featured, Recently Viewed):** Functional label, 2-4 words. *"Featured Research"* · *"Top Downloads"* · *"Trending This Quarter"*.
- **Eyebrow labels:** ALL CAPS, 10-11px, tracking-wider. *"INDUSTRY"* · *"PUBLISHED Q1 2026"* · *"125 PAGES"*.
- **Filter labels:** Sentence case, 12-14px. *"Industry"* · *"Published Year"* · *"Page Count"* · *"Region"*.

---

## Body rules

- **Card descriptions:** 1-2 sentences max, ~120 chars. State scope + value, not opinion.
- **Report previews:** Structured metadata, not paragraphs. Format: *Pages · Charts · Segments · Date · Region · Authors*.
- **Methodology blurbs:** 2-3 sentences. State approach + sample size + date.
- **Voice:** Almost entirely active. Present tense for current data. Past tense only for "Published 2024" type metadata.
- **Pronouns:** Avoid first-person almost entirely. No "we", no "you" except in CTAs and survey prompts.
- **Lists over prose:** When listing scope, segments, regions — always bullets or comma-separated, never narrative paragraphs.
- **Em-dash usage:** Sparing. Use for metadata separators on cards (*"125 pages — Published Q1 2026 — India"*).

---

## CTA copy rules

- **Action-led, specific.** Never "click here", never "learn more" alone.
- **2-3 words ideal.** Max 4.
- **GOOD:** *"View Report"* · *"Download Sample"* · *"Request TOC"* · *"Get Pricing"* · *"View Methodology"*
- **BAD:** *"Click to read more!"* · *"Discover insights now"* · *"Unlock the full report"* · *"Learn what's inside"*
- **No "Free", "Now", "Today"** unless commercially required (e.g. lead-magnet form).

---

## Number / data formatting

- **Stats:** Always numerals. *"412 respondents"* not *"four hundred twelve"*.
- **Currencies:** ISO + amount. *"USD 2.4M"* / *"INR 18 Cr"*. Match audience expectation.
- **CAGR / percentages:** Numeral + `%` no space + period range in parentheses. *"CAGR 8.4% (2024–2029)"*.
- **Year ranges:** En-dash. *"2024–2029"* not *"2024-2029"* and not *"2024 to 2029"*.
- **Page count:** Numeral + " pages". *"125 pages"* not *"125pp"*.
- **Date format:** Quarter + year. *"Q1 2026"* / *"Published Q3 2025"*. Avoid full dates unless precise (release-date contexts).
- **Filter counts:** Parentheses, numeral. *"Healthcare (47)"* — count after label.
- **Sort options:** Sentence case, descriptive. *"Newest first"* / *"Most downloaded"* / *"Title A-Z"*.

---

## Anti-patterns

1. **Never use "the only", "the first", "the best"** without verifiable claim source. Research voice loses trust on hyperbole.
2. **Never use exclamation marks.** Period.
3. **Never use second-person promotional language** ("your competitive edge", "your business", "your team"). Research = neutral catalog, not pitch.
4. **Never abbreviate report titles.** Full publication name always.
5. **Never use vague time references** ("recently", "soon", "shortly"). Use exact: *"Published Q1 2026"* / *"Updated weekly"* / *"Refreshes Mondays"*.
6. **Never use "powered by AI" or "AI-driven"** as a value prop. Methodology specificity matters more than buzzword.
7. **Never use sentence-fragment headlines like ad copy** ("Insights that drive growth"). Headlines = labels, not slogans.
8. **Never use emoji in copy.** Ever.
9. **Never write "this report covers everything"** — claim coverage with specifics ("12 sectors, 6 regions, 200+ pages").
10. **Never bury methodology.** Always 1-click reachable from any report card.
11. **Never use "comprehensive"** — empty filler. Replace with specifics.
12. **Never use "actionable insights"** — meaningless cliché. Say what the insight enables.
