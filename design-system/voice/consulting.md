# Voice — Consulting Pillar

**Color tag:** black / neutral, Ken Red `#b01f24` for CTAs only  
**Lives on:** Case studies, advisory pages, methodology showcases, engagement narratives, client outcome pages

---

## Identity

The advisor's voice. Calm authority. Earned by methodology, not adjectives.

---

## When to use this surface

- Client engagement showcases (case studies)
- Methodology documentation (how Ken Research works)
- Strategic advisory pages
- Outcome-led narratives (impact, transformation, results)
- Service overview pages
- Premium positioning content

**NOT for:** report listings (use Research voice), survey product pages (use Surveys voice), internal DS docs (use Foundations voice).

---

## Tone

**3 adjectives:** Authoritative · Editorial · Restrained.  
**1 forbidden adjective:** Salesy.

The Consulting reader is a senior buyer (CHRO, Strategy Head, Founder, GM). They reject promotional copy and respect understatement. Voice stands on outcomes, not claims.

---

## Vocabulary

**Allowed (use these):**
mandate · architecture · framework · transformation · alignment · workforce · stakeholder · methodology · diagnosis · intervention · outcome · cadence · governance · operating model · commercial · structural · systemic · participation · adoption · shift

**Forbidden (avoid):**
revolutionary · game-changing · disrupt · cutting-edge · synergy · best-in-class · leverage (as verb) · world-class · innovative · seamless · empower (overused) · unlock potential · journey (cliché) · unleash · supercharge · turbocharge · level up · pivot · ideate

---

## Sentence patterns

**GOOD:**
> Empowering a 60,000+ workforce through intelligent alignment, human-centric design, and data-driven insights.

**BAD:**
> We empower your global team to unlock world-class workforce transformation and seamlessly drive synergies.

**GOOD:**
> A diversified manufacturing group facing a critical disengagement crisis among its frontline workforce.

**BAD:**
> A leading enterprise tackling employee engagement challenges to revolutionize their HR operations.

**GOOD:**
> Designed a customized engagement framework tailored for blue-collar ecosystems.

**BAD:**
> Delivered a cutting-edge solution that transformed how the client thinks about engagement.

---

## Reading level target

Flesch-Kincaid Grade 11-13. Educated executive reader. Trades simplicity for precision.

Sentences may run 25-35 words. Subordinate clauses OK. Avoid bullet-point fragmentation when prose serves better.

---

## Headline rules

- **Hero h1:** 2-4 words MAX, declarative. Often a noun phrase + verb past tense. Example: *"Industrial Excellence Redefined."* / *"Operations Reimagined."* / *"Culture Restored."*
- **Section h2:** 3-6 words. Often noun + qualifier + period. Example: *"Strategic Mandates."* / *"The Transformation Architecture."* / *"Tangible Impact."*
- **Eyebrow labels:** ALL CAPS, tracking-wide, 9-11px. Example: *"CHAPTER 02 — STRATEGIC OBJECTIVES"* / *"PHASE BETA"*
- **Use serif italic** for emphasis word in mixed-weight headlines. Example: *"The Transformation Architecture."* (italic on "Transformation")

---

## Body rules

- **Paragraph length:** 2-4 sentences typical. Single-sentence paragraphs allowed for emphasis.
- **Density:** Prose-led. Em-dashes for parenthetical thought (`—`, never `--` or `-`).
- **Voice:** Mostly active. Past tense for client work (showed outcome). Present tense for capability (current methodology).
- **Pronouns:** "We" for Ken Research. "The client" or named industry ("manufacturer", "logistics group") for client. Avoid "you" except in CTAs.
- **Numbers:** Spell out 1-9 in prose. Numerals for 10+. Always numerals for stats and percentages.

---

## CTA copy rules

- **Verb-led, declarative.** Never questions. Never exclamations.
- **2-3 words ideal.** Max 4.
- **GOOD:** *"Initiate Strategy"* · *"Discuss Engagement"* · *"Request Briefing"* · *"Read Methodology"*
- **BAD:** *"Get Started!"* · *"Click Here to Learn More"* · *"Book Your Free Consultation Today!"* · *"Let's Talk"*
- **No emoji. Ever.** No exclamation marks. No exclamatory adjectives ("incredible", "amazing").

---

## Number / data formatting

- **Stats above 100:** Use comma separator. *"60,000+ employees"* not *"60000+"*.
- **Percentages:** Numeral + `%` no space. *"34%"* not *"34 %"* or *"thirty-four percent"*.
- **Time ranges:** En-dash. *"2024–2026"* not *"2024-2026"*.
- **Currencies:** ISO + amount. *"USD 2.4M"* not *"$2.4 million"* (international audience).
- **K, M, B:** Capital. *"60K"* / *"2.4M"* / *"1.2B"*.
- **Animation:** Stat counters animate from 0 to target on scroll-in. Reduced-motion shows final state immediately.

---

## Anti-patterns

1. **Never use exclamation marks.** Period.
2. **Never use "journey" as metaphor.** Cliché. Say "engagement", "process", "transformation".
3. **Never use second-person sales language** ("you'll discover", "your dream", "your potential").
4. **Never use "powered by", "driven by", "fueled by"** — meaningless filler.
5. **Never use weather/sports metaphors** ("storm", "winning", "championship", "marathon").
6. **Never use industry acronyms unexplained.** Define on first use.
7. **Never write claims without methodology link.** "We achieved 34% reduction" = always include "through structured X" or page link to methodology.
8. **Never use "innovative" or "innovation"** as a positive descriptor — overused, hollow.
9. **Never use "transform" as a verb in headings.** Use as noun phrase. *"Transformation Architecture"* OK. *"We transform your business"* not OK.
10. **Never apologize, hedge, or qualify** in the lead. *"This is one approach among many"* = weak. *"This is the framework that worked"* = correct.
