# Voice — Surveys Pillar

**Color tag:** purple `#806ce0` accent on editorial light, Ken Red `#b01f24` for CTAs only  
**Lives on:** Survey listings, survey detail pages, lifecycle states (open / closing-soon / closed / completed), completion flows, response confirmations

---

## Identity

The host's voice. Welcoming, time-respecting, completion-focused. Lowers cognitive load. Earns trust through transparency about effort and outcome.

---

## When to use this surface

- Survey listing pages
- Individual survey detail / take-survey pages
- Survey lifecycle states (open / closing soon / completed / archived)
- Question preview cards
- Completion confirmation pages
- Methodology / privacy disclosure on surveys
- Response chart previews

**NOT for:** report listings (use Research voice), case studies (use Consulting voice), DS docs (use Foundations voice).

---

## Tone

**3 adjectives:** Welcoming · Transparent · Brief.  
**1 forbidden adjective:** Pushy.

The Surveys reader is being asked to give time. Voice respects that with: clear time estimates upfront, transparent privacy stance, no manipulation, easy exit.

---

## Vocabulary

**Allowed (use these):**
participate · respondent · response · question · section · time estimate · anonymous · confidential · voluntary · submit · skip · save progress · resume · close · open · participate · completion rate · sample · methodology · privacy · open until · closes · completed · published

**Forbidden (avoid):**
hurry · last chance · don't miss · only X spots left · exclusive · VIP · members-only · limited time (manipulative) · urgent · act fast · few left · final hours

---

## Sentence patterns

**GOOD:**
> 12 questions · 5 minutes · Anonymous.

**BAD:**
> Take this important survey now! Don't miss your chance!

**GOOD:**
> Open until 30 May 2026. 1,247 responses so far.

**BAD:**
> Closing soon — last chance to participate!

**GOOD:**
> Your responses are anonymous and used only for aggregate analysis.

**BAD:**
> We promise we won't share your data with anyone, ever, we swear!

**GOOD:**
> Skip any question. Save and resume later.

**BAD:**
> Please complete every question to ensure your voice is heard!

---

## Reading level target

Flesch-Kincaid Grade 7-9. Lower than Research and Consulting. Survey context = many readers, varied literacy levels, often mobile-first.

Sentences short. Single-clause. Direct.

---

## Headline rules

- **Survey card title:** 3-8 words, descriptive of subject. *"Frontline Workforce Engagement 2026"* · *"GCC Healthcare Sentiment"* · *"Mid-Market HR Operations Pulse"*.
- **Survey detail page h1:** Match card title exactly. No marketing rewrite.
- **Section h2 inside survey detail:** Functional. *"What we ask"* · *"Time required"* · *"How responses are used"* · *"Privacy"*.
- **Eyebrow labels:** ALL CAPS, 10-11px. *"OPEN"* · *"CLOSES IN 5 DAYS"* · *"COMPLETED"* · *"DRAFT"*.
- **Lifecycle badges:** Single word + state. *"Open"* (purple) · *"Closing soon"* (amber) · *"Closed"* (grey) · *"Completed"* (success-themed).
- **Question section headers:** Sentence case, brief. *"About your role"* · *"Your team's tools"* · *"Final thoughts"*.

---

## Body rules

- **Question text:** Question-form, max 20 words. *"How many people report to you directly?"* not *"Please indicate the precise number of direct reports under your management hierarchy."*
- **Help text:** Sub-question clarification, 1 sentence. *"Include only direct reports, not skip-level."*
- **Privacy / disclosure:** Stated up front, plain language. *"Anonymous. Aggregated. Not shared."* — not buried legalese.
- **Voice:** Direct, second-person ("you") for questions and prompts. First-person plural ("we") sparingly when describing methodology.
- **Time estimates:** Always upfront, conservative. Round up: *"5 minutes"* not *"4.7 minutes"*. Better to over-deliver.
- **Progress indicators:** Show position. *"Question 4 of 12"* / *"Section 2 of 4"*. Always reachable.

---

## CTA copy rules

- **Verb-led, low pressure.**
- **2-3 words.**
- **GOOD:** *"Start Survey"* · *"Skip Question"* · *"Save Progress"* · *"Resume Later"* · *"Submit Responses"* · *"View Methodology"*
- **BAD:** *"Take it now!"* · *"Don't miss out"* · *"Hurry — closes soon!"* · *"Submit your voice"*
- **Closing-soon framing:** Factual not urgent. *"Closes 30 May"* not *"Last chance!"*. Let the date do the urgency.
- **Completion CTA:** Acknowledge the time given. *"Thank you. Your response is recorded."*

---

## Number / data formatting

- **Time estimates:** Round, conservative. *"5 minutes"* / *"under 10 minutes"* / *"approximately 15 minutes"*.
- **Question counts:** Numeral + " questions". *"12 questions"*.
- **Response counts:** Comma separator. *"1,247 responses"*.
- **Completion rate:** Numeral + `%`. *"68% completion rate"* — only show if calibration matters (methodology context).
- **Date format:** Day Month Year for closing dates. *"Closes 30 May 2026"*.
- **Sample sizes:** Show source location. *"412 respondents · 6 cities · India"*.

---

## Anti-patterns

1. **Never use scarcity manipulation.** No "only X spots left" / "limited time" / "members only" framing.
2. **Never use exclamation marks.** Survey voice = calm host, not infomercial.
3. **Never bury time estimate.** Time required = top of card and detail page, before question 1.
4. **Never bury privacy stance.** Anonymous / confidential / voluntary = visible before any data collection starts.
5. **Never use "important" or "critical"** to describe a survey unsolicited. Reader decides importance.
6. **Never use "your voice matters"** — empty platitude. Replace with concrete: *"Aggregated for the 2026 GCC HR Outlook report"*.
7. **Never use "quick" or "easy"** to describe survey effort. Just state time. *"5 minutes"* > *"quick survey"*.
8. **Never use questions in CTAs.** *"Want to participate?"* = bad. *"Start Survey"* = good.
9. **Never make questions optional but mark required.** If skippable, label clearly.
10. **Never use jargon in questions** without inline definition.
11. **Never use 7-point Likert scales.** 5-point max for cognitive load. Reference scale in DS molecule.
12. **Never break commitment about anonymity.** If survey is identified, label it clearly and never claim "anonymous".
